How ReCity AI Processes Submissions

  • 1. Spatial Morphology: Computes available footprint and buffers.
  • 2. Need Matching: Cross-references ward-level deficits (e.g. green deficiency or child play deficit).
  • 3. 3-Futures Engine: Creates Concept A (Garden), Concept B (Play), Concept C (Learning Hub).
  • 4. Cost Schedule: Generates CapEx, OpEx, and Tactical Phase 1 budget estimates.
